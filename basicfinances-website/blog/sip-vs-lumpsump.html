SIP vs Lumpsum

Which investment method suits you?

Introduction: Investors often get confused between SIP and lump sum investments. Let’s simplify.

What is SIP?

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) allows you to invest a fixed amount regularly.

What is Lumpsum?

Lumpsum investment means investing a large amount at one time.

Which One Should You Choose?

Your choice depends on market conditions, risk appetite, and financial goals.

Conclusion: SIP suits long-term disciplined investors, while lump sum works when markets are undervalued.
